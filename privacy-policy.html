Privacy Policy

Your privacy is important to us. Learn how we protect your information.

1. Introduction

Welcome to Helpy Anand Tools. We are committed to maintaining the privacy and security of your personal information. This Privacy Policy outlines the types of information we collect, how it is used, and the measures we take to safeguard your data.

2. File Processing & Data Privacy (No Server Uploads)

Your privacy is our highest priority. All tool utilities provided on this site (such as Image to PDF Conversion) perform file processing directly inside your client-side browser using JavaScript. We do not upload, collect, store, or transmit your images or files to any external server. Once you close or refresh your browser tab, your processed files are cleared from browser memory.

3. Information We Collect

We do not collect personally identifiable information (PII) unless you voluntarily provide it to us through our contact forms or email inquiries.

4. Log Files

Helpy Anand Tools follows standard website practices by using log files. These files log visitors when they visit websites. The information collected by log files includes internet protocol (IP) addresses, browser type, Internet Service Provider (ISP), date/time stamp, referring/exit pages, and click counts. This data is not linked to any personally identifiable information and is used strictly for analyzing trends and site administration.

5. Cookies and Web Beacons

Like any other website, Helpy Anand Tools uses 'cookies' to store preferences and optimize user experience. You can choose to disable cookies through your individual browser options.

6. Third-Party Advertisers (Google AdSense & Analytics)

We may use third-party service providers and advertising partners on our website, such as Google AdSense and Microsoft Clarity analytics:

  • Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies to serve ads on our site based on users' visits to this and other sites on the internet.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting the Google Ads Settings.
  • Analytical networks (like Microsoft Clarity) help us understand website traffic patterns and improve performance.

7. Children's Privacy Information

Protecting children's privacy online is paramount. Helpy Anand Tools does not knowingly collect any Personal Identifiable Information from children under the age of 13.

8. Consent

By using our website, you hereby consent to our Privacy Policy and agree to its terms.

9. Contact Us

If you have additional questions or require more information about our Privacy Policy, please feel free to reach out via our Contact Page.
