What Is an AI Resume & ATS Checker?

An AI Resume & ATS Checker helps job seekers evaluate their resume before applying for a job. It checks important resume sections, keywords, formatting and compatibility with Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS).

By comparing your resume with a job description, you can identify missing skills and keywords that may be important for the position.

How Does an ATS Resume Checker Work?

Applicant Tracking Systems are commonly used by employers to organize and filter job applications. A resume checker analyzes the text and structure of your resume and identifies areas that may need improvement.

  1. Upload your resume.
  2. Add the job description.
  3. Analyze your resume.
  4. Review the ATS score.
  5. Improve your resume using the suggestions.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is an ATS?

ATS stands for Applicant Tracking System. Companies use ATS software to organize and manage job applications.

Is this Resume Checker free?

Yes. The AI Resume & ATS Checker is designed as a free online tool for checking resume compatibility.

Which resume formats are supported?

The tool is designed to support PDF, DOCX and TXT resume files.

Can I check my resume against a job description?

Yes. Paste the job description into the Job Description box to compare important keywords and requirements.

Is my resume uploaded to a server?

Our goal is to process resume content directly in your browser whenever possible, helping minimize unnecessary data transmission.
