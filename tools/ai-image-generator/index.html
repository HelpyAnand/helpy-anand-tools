How to Write Better AI Image Prompts

A clear and detailed prompt can help describe your desired image more accurately. Include details about the subject, background, lighting, colors, image style and composition. For example, instead of writing "a village", describe "a peaceful Indian village at sunrise with green fields and warm golden sunlight".
