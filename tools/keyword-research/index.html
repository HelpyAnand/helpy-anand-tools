KEYWORD RESEARCH GUIDE

How to Find the Right Keywords

Keyword research helps you understand the phrases people use when searching for information, products and services. Use keyword ideas to plan useful content and improve your website's search visibility.

What Are Long-tail Keywords?

Long-tail keywords are specific search phrases that usually contain more words. They can help you target focused topics and answer more specific search needs.

What Are Question Keywords?

Question keywords are searches that begin with words such as what, how, why, when and where. They can help you discover topics for guides, tutorials and FAQs.

What Is Search Intent?

Search intent describes the likely purpose behind a search. It is commonly grouped into informational, commercial, transactional and navigational categories.

How to Use Keyword Clusters

Keyword clustering groups related phrases together. These groups can help you plan website categories, articles and topic-focused content.
